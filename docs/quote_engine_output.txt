Tick-driven quote engine, three 50,000-tick sessions with distinct feed
seeds, produced by scripts/run_quote_sessions.sh 3 50000 50 3000. Each
session: quote_feed_sender -> UDP multicast (239.255.0.1:30301) -> a
pinned-core quote_engine_live receiver, started first and given 1s to
bind and join the multicast group before the sender starts; immediately
followed by quote_engine_replay against that session's own capture file.

Summary: p99 ranged 8.914us to 11.817us across the three sessions (all
under the 15us target), on 1 physical core pinned out of 12 (AMD Ryzen 7
7800X3D), WSL2 Ubuntu 22.04, this machine otherwise in ordinary use.
Session 2's higher p99 (11.817us) and higher max (126.6us, a single
outlier) look like ordinary contention noise from other processes on a
shared machine, not a different code path; all three sessions received
every one of their 50,000 ticks (0 drops on this loopback multicast path
at 50us pacing) and all three live checksums match their own session's
replay checksum exactly, and differ from each other (different seeds
produced genuinely different tick sequences), which is the evidence that
the checksum is actually sensitive to the data, not a constant.
